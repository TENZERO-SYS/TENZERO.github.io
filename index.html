ACT I / Preparation & Reconnaissance

Know the grid before you act.

Build situational awareness, establish safe positions, and scout before committing to a recovery.

PHASE 01

Situational Awareness

Know your location before you act. Check your current system, review local chat, and identify who is present. Gather information before making your next move.

PHASE 02

Safe Spots & Reconnaissance

Establish safe spots where you can cloak and observe your surroundings. Use them to scout activity, monitor threats, and plan your next move without exposing your ship unnecessarily.

PHASE 03

Gate Reconnaissance

Once your location is known and safe spots are established, scout gates and monitor nearby activity. If gankers land on grid, do not react immediately. Observe their movements and assess the situation before committing.

ACT II / Engagement & Recovery

Observe. Recover. Extract.

Wait for the right moment, recover assets quickly, and leave before the situation turns against you.

PHASE 01

Observe & Confirm

When gankers land on grid, watch as they engage their intended target and become criminally flagged. Do not intervene yet. Track the attackers and let the situation develop before making your move.

PHASE 02

Rapid Response & Loot Recovery

Time is critical. Prepare a fitted frigate with a fast align time so you can respond quickly and enter a suspect state when looting yellow wrecks. Coordinate with fellow pilots; tractor beams can help move blue wrecks away from the gankers’ looting hauler and create recovery opportunities.

PHASE 03

Extract, Secure & Reset

Get out as quickly as possible. If you loot a yellow wreck and become suspect-flagged, the gankers’ other ships may legally engage you. Stay pre-aligned and warp out as soon as you have secured the loot. Dock up, assess the recovered assets, and wait for your suspect timer to expire before preparing for another opportunity.
